The rules behind the labels

These simplified definitions are transparent chart-study heuristics. They are not FXSynapse’s proprietary AI or a forecast.

Break of structure (BOS)
A close crosses the most recent confirmed swing high or low. A swing requires two lower highs or higher lows on both sides and is not available until those right-hand candles have closed. Each swing is broken once.
Fair value gap (FVG)
Three-candle gap: the third candle’s low exceeds the first candle’s high, or its high falls below the first candle’s low. These are historical events; the app does not track whether gaps were later filled.
Candidate order block
The full high–low range of the last opposite-color candle within eight candles before a BOS. This is one heuristic, not proof of institutional orders.
Liquidity sweep
A wick exceeds the most recent unbroken confirmed swing level, then the same candle closes back on the other side.

All input candles are treated as closed. CSV time must increase. Prices must be positive and OHLC ranges valid. No broker or AI provider is contacted.
